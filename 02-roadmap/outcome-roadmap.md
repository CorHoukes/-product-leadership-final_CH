## 2. Trade-off memo

> **Rock #1:** Simplify the daily log: reduce from 14 required fields to 4 -> Reduce time spent creating weekly project status reports by 30%
>
> **Rock #2:** Enable push notifications for schedule changes -> Increase percentage of project communication captured within Meridian from 25% to 40%.
>
> **Rock #3:** Add a time-tracking module -> Increase active field users by 50% in the next quarter
>
> **Hard No #1:** Refresh web UI: Low urgency unless data shows it is affecting conversion or adoption. Cosmetic improvements typically generate less value than workflow improvements.
>
> **Hard No #2:** Build an automated compliance checklist generator High effort (4), low impact (2). Only requested by a small subset of enterprise prospects. Could potentially be addressed through configuration or partnerships instead of custom development.
>
> **Hard No #3:** Fix the Procore import/export integration (broken for 2 clients) Impact score may be underestimated. Existing customer pain affecting 2 clients.
