# Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** centralized project information, communication and governance platform for construction projects.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** For Superintendents, foremen and site managers in large enterprise construction companies and/or project portfolios >75M in USA
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** 10 years project controls solution and documentation management with structured data storage + auto feed dashboarding capabilities
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** intuitive and interaction (phone, photo, text) construction project management platform. From floor actions to platform insights.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** weekly project status progress and budget insights, % document compliance completeness, Monthly Active Field User (+ foremen and superintendents)

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** Will not extend to the regular in company project management solutions like Jira or Microsoft Project

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Make Meridian the preferred platform for construction projects
- **KR1:** Increase active field users by 50% in the next quarter
- **KR2:** Reduce off-platform communication by 25% in the next qaurter
- **KR3:** Increase automatically captured project updates by 25% in the next quarter
